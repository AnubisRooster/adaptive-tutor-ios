Manage your data

Adaptive Tutor keeps your data on your device. This page explains how to review and delete it.

Where your data lives

Everything — profiles, answers, progress, imported notes, and your optional API key — is stored locally on your device in the App's database and secure storage. We do not operate servers, so there is nothing to delete from us.

Review or export your data

Open the App and select Progress to review mastery, XP, streaks, and levels for the active profile. Learning content and saved sources are visible in Learn and searchable with the Search feature. There is no separate export feature; the data lives in a local database on your device.

Delete a single profile

  1. Open Settings in the App.
  2. Select the profile and choose Delete profile.
  3. This removes that profile's locally stored messages, progress, settings, and secure items — including its API key, if one was stored.

Delete everything

  • Delete each profile in Settings, or
  • Uninstall the App. Uninstalling removes the App's local database and secure storage from your device.

Tracking and advertising

Adaptive Tutor does not run ads or third-party tracking, and does not sell your data. If you enabled Cloud mode, your prompts are sent to the OpenRouter provider you configured only for the requests you make.
