Adaptive Tutor Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

In one line: Adaptive Tutor is a private, local-first study app. By default everything you type and learn stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no trackers, and no servers run by us. An optional Cloud mode lets you bring your own OpenRouter API key, in which case the prompts you choose to send go to that provider.

1. Who we are

Adaptive Tutor ("we", "the App") is an iOS study and tutoring application that uses AI models to teach subjects, generate lessons and quizzes, and track your mastery. This policy explains what information the App processes, where it is stored, and what happens in Cloud mode.

2. Information you provide

  • Profile name. Stored only on your device to label learning profiles (e.g. "Maya").
  • Passcode / PIN and biometric lock. If you enable a lock, the PIN and your Face ID / Touch ID preference are stored using the secure storage provided by your device (iOS Keychain). Biometric data itself is never readable by the App — it is handled entirely by iOS.
  • Learning activity. Your answers, quiz responses, grading results, streaks, XP, mastery levels, and achievements are stored in a local database on your device.
  • Imported study material. If you add a note or ingest content from a URL, that content is retrieved and stored locally on your device so Search and tutoring can use it offline.
  • Optional OpenRouter API key. If you enable Cloud mode, the App stores the API key you paste in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain). The key is never sent to us — it is used only to authenticate requests you make to OpenRouter directly from the App.
  • Study reminders. If you enable reminders, the schedule is stored locally and notifications are generated on your device. Push notifications are not used.

3. Information collected automatically

The App does not run our own analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs, and does not send information to us. You may separately receive standard App Store diagnostics (for example, delivered by Apple when you report a problem or when crash diagnostics are enabled in iOS Settings). Apple's handling of such diagnostics is governed by Apple's own policies, not this one.

4. On-Device mode (default)

By default the App runs entirely on your device using a downloaded AI model (built on llama.cpp with the Metal GPU backend). In On-Device mode nothing you type, ask, or learn is sent over the network. Lessons, quizzes, and grades are generated locally, and all data remains in the App's local database and secure storage.

5. Cloud mode (optional, bring-your-own-key)

Cloud mode is optional and is never assumed. When you switch the LLM provider to OpenRouter in Settings, the App asks you to confirm a consent notice before Cloud mode becomes active. In Cloud mode:

  • Your prompts and answers are sent to OpenRouter (openrouter.ai) to generate tutoring responses and grades, using your own API key.
  • OpenRouter passes the content to the model provider you select (for example, Google Gemma). Those providers have their own privacy policies, which you can review before choosing a model.
  • We do not see, store, sell, or use your prompts. The App itself never transmits anything on your behalf other than the requests you initiate to the provider you configured.

You can turn Cloud mode off at any time in Settings. Profiles and progress stay on your device regardless of which mode is active.

6. How we share information

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.
  • The only data that leaves your device is what you explicitly send to OpenRouter (and onward to a model provider) in Cloud mode, plus routine network requests the App makes on your behalf to the provider you selected.

7. Retention

All of your data lives on your device. We do not store copies of your data anywhere, so there is no retention period beyond the lifetime of the data on your device. An API key stored for a profile remains in your device's secure storage until you delete it or delete the profile.

8. Deleting your data

  • Deleting a profile in the App removes that profile's locally stored messages, progress, settings, and secure items (including the API key, if one was stored).
  • You can delete individual profiles or all data by removing the App from your device (uninstall deletes the App's local database and secure storage).

9. Children's privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

The App is designed for general audiences aged 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because data is stored locally and is not transmitted unless you explicitly enable Cloud mode, we hold no personal information about any user, including minors. Parental or guardian guidance is recommended for learners under 18.

10. Security

Sensitive items such as your API key, PIN, and biometric lock setting are stored in the iOS Keychain via secure storage provided by the platform. Learning data is stored in a local database on your device. The App runs no servers, so there is no remote database that could be exposed. As with any device, we recommend keeping your device locked and up to date.

11. AI-generated content

Tutoring responses, lessons, quizzes, and grades are generated by an AI model and may occasionally be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable for your goals. AI content is a study aid, not a substitute for professional medical, legal, or financial advice. The App surfaces this disclosure at setup and in its in-app Privacy & AI Disclosure screen.

12. Your rights and international users

Because we do not collect, store, or process your personal data on systems we operate, there is nothing for you to access, export, correct, or erase from us — deletion happens entirely inside the App (see section 8). If you are in a jurisdiction with data-protection laws (such as the GDPR or UK GDPR), this design means the App performs no "controller" processing of your data beyond what you keep on your own device.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when the App's features change. Updates will be reflected in this document with a new "Effective date" above. The in-app Privacy & AI Disclosure screen will be kept consistent with this policy.

14. Contact

Questions about privacy or this policy? Contact us at privacy@adaptivetutor.example.
